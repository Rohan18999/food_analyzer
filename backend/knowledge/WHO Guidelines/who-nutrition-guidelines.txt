WHO dietary guidelines emphasize a balanced diet rich in fruits, vegetables, whole grains, legumes, and nuts. Limiting free sugars to less than 10% of total daily energy intake is recommended, with additional benefits from reducing to below 5%.

WHO recommends limiting saturated fat to less than 10% of total energy intake and trans fat to less than 1%. Replacing saturated fats with unsaturated fats reduces the risk of heart disease.

Adults should consume at least 25 grams of dietary fiber per day from whole food sources. Adequate fiber intake supports digestive health, blood sugar regulation, and cardiovascular health.

Processed foods high in sugar, salt, and unhealthy fats should be minimized in favor of minimally processed whole foods.
