The FDA recommends a Daily Value of 2,300 mg of sodium per day for adults. On nutrition labels, sodium content is listed in milligrams (mg) per serving along with the percent Daily Value (%DV).

FDA guidance for interpreting sodium levels per serving:
- 5% DV or less = low sodium
- 20% DV or more = high sodium

The FDA also requires added sugars to be listed separately on nutrition labels. Products with high added sugars should be consumed in moderation. The Daily Value for added sugars is 50 grams per day.

Food manufacturers must follow FDA labeling standards for serving sizes, nutrient declarations, and ingredient lists in descending order by weight.
