General nutrition recommendations for evaluating packaged food products:

1. Check the ingredient list — shorter lists with recognizable whole foods are generally better.
2. Prioritize products with higher protein and fiber per serving.
3. Limit products high in sodium (>600mg), added sugars (>10g), and saturated fat (>3g) per serving.
4. Be cautious of products containing artificial trans fats, excessive preservatives, and refined grains as primary ingredients.
5. Compare similar products and choose options with better nutrient profiles.

When evaluating snacks and convenience foods, look for whole grain ingredients, nuts, seeds, and minimal added sugars. Homemade alternatives often provide better nutritional control.
