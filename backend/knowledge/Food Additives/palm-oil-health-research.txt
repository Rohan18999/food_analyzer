Palm oil is a widely used vegetable oil in processed foods due to its stability and low cost. It is high in saturated fat, containing approximately 50% saturated fatty acids.

Health concerns associated with palm oil:
- High saturated fat content may raise LDL ("bad") cholesterol levels
- Linked to increased cardiovascular disease risk when consumed in excess
- Often used in ultra-processed snack foods, instant noodles, and baked goods

The WHO recommends limiting saturated fat intake. Products listing palm oil as a primary ingredient, especially near the top of the ingredient list, tend to be higher in saturated fat.

Healthier alternatives include products made with unsaturated oils such as olive oil, sunflower oil, or canola oil.
