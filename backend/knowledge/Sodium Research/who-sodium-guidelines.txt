The World Health Organization recommends that adults consume less than 2,000 mg of sodium per day (equivalent to 5 grams of salt). High sodium intake is linked to elevated blood pressure, increased risk of heart disease, and stroke.

For processed and packaged foods, WHO advises choosing products with lower sodium content. Products exceeding 600mg sodium per serving are considered high-sodium. Consuming multiple high-sodium products throughout the day can easily exceed daily limits.

Reducing sodium intake by choosing fresh foods, cooking at home, and reading nutrition labels can significantly improve cardiovascular health.
