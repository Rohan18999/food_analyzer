Saturated fat research indicates a strong association between high saturated fat intake and elevated LDL cholesterol, a major risk factor for coronary heart disease.

Evidence-based recommendations:
- Limit saturated fat to less than 10% of daily caloric intake
- Foods high in saturated fat include fatty meats, full-fat dairy, butter, coconut oil, and palm oil
- Replacing saturated fats with polyunsaturated and monounsaturated fats reduces cardiovascular risk
- A product with more than 3g saturated fat per serving is considered moderately high; above 5g is high

The type of fat matters as much as total fat. Processed snacks, fried foods, and baked goods often contain significant saturated fat from oils and dairy ingredients.
